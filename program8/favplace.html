MY FAVOURITE PLACE


Munnar

Munnar is one of South India's premier hill stations, situated at an altitude of approximately 1,600 meters (5,200 ft) in the Western Ghats. The name Munnar translates to "three rivers" in Malayalam, referring to its location at the confluence of the Muthirapuzha, Nallathanni, and Kundala rivers.

The landscape is characterized by winding lanes, scenic viewpoints, and rich flora and fauna, including the endangered Nilgiri Tahr found in nearby Eravikulam National Park and the rare Neelakurinji flower, which blankets the hills in blue every twelve years.
